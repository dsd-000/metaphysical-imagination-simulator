METAPHYSICAL IMAGINATION SIMULATOR

Metaphysics, or the study of how reality exists, has been predominately expressed linguistically in the Western canon -- the stroke of the artist belittled as unworthy for fully rational thought. But what of their imagination, these philosophers for whom thinking was only to be articulated in the linguistic mode? Should we take them at their word, that their philosophies are but propositions?

Or should we see in them an expression of something which would not be limited to the materials before them -- what is captured as much by a literal reading of their language as it is by a latent pathway of feeling and visual content erupting as the in-between of technical definitions, rhetorical flourish, and emergent constraints? Though there is a more recent trend of philosophers taking seriously the possibility of achieving metaphysical apperception through diagrammatic means, I have always felt I do not understand any philosophical system unless I can grasp it in my mind's eye so to speak.

That is, by imagining a mobile geometry which is partially seeded in my surroundings and yet also persistently just beyond, a spatial rendering of abstractions into a form vibrant and mutable to the transformation procedures the philosopher professes.

TWO-FOLD PURPOSE
01
To assist in making the aesthetic and structural intuitions underlying philosophical systems explicit by generating a fluid, visual depiction of their requisite dynamics.

To enable users to practically explore the pluripotent representational capacities latent within generic philosophical categories.

CREATION PATHS

All three paths use object-oriented programming under the hood. The choice is philosophical -- it reflects what you take to be ontologically primary in your world.

SUBSTANCE
Entity classes first

Define what kinds of things exist before specifying anything else. Each entity class has intrinsic properties and behaviours. Best for Leibniz, Harman, Spinoza.

EXAMPLE
class Monad:
    grade: Grade = BARE
    frequency: float

    @each_frame
    def unfold(self, world, dt):
        self.orbit()
RELATIONAL
Interaction rules first

Define what happens when kinds of things meet. An entity's nature is constituted entirely by its relations. Best for Peirce, Brassier, Particle Life-style ontologies.

RULE MATRIX
Attractor + Attractor -> orbit
Attractor + Void      -> attract
Void      + Void      -> repel
Body      + Attractor -> scatter
PROCESS
Observe and name

Define nothing in advance. Run the simulation and name stable patterns you observe in the Named Configurations bar. Best for Whitehead, Bergson, Deleuze.

WHAT YOU DO
1. Hit RUN SIMULATION
2. Watch what emerges
3. Name stable patterns:
   "resonance_cluster"
   "dispersal_wave"
4. Tag to producing context
